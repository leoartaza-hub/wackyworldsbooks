Meet the five

Five worlds, five skills, five unlikely friends. Together they're the strangest (and best) crew at Crazy Space Academy.

Lexi Ollix Astro SUM-IX Arti & Pompita
THE DREAMY STORYTELLER · LETTERS & WORDS

Lexi

“I read and leap where stories sleep.”

Lexi is a little lime-green frog who loves reading: comics, novels, fairy tales, even superhero manuals. So much, in fact, that he believes he is every character he reads about. One day he's a ninja turtle, the next a medieval knight. Dramatic, brave and sweet, his motto is simple: “If I read it… then it could be real.”

THE VISUAL MIND · OBSERVATION

Ollix

“I see the small, I watch it all.”

Ollix is a floating turquoise octopus with lots of eyes. They never agree on where to look, but they always end up being right. Calm, clever and a little bit sarcastic, Ollix sees EVERYTHING, even the things nobody wants to see. Her favourite line: “That was there from the very beginning.”

THE CHAOTIC EXPLORER · MAZES & MAPS

Astro

“I roam the stars just as they are.”

Astro is a big, noble Saint Bernard in a cosmonaut suit and a pirate space helmet. He loves maps, paths and treasure, and follows his nose everywhere. Adventurous, clumsy and endlessly optimistic, he always gets lost… and always discovers something: “I don't know where we are… but it's definitely interesting!”

THE MATH BRAIN · NUMBERS & LOGIC

SUM-IX

“I count and care, I solve and share.”

SUM-IX is a compact red robot with an LED chest full of numbers and arms shaped like rulers. When he gets confused, silly equations pop up on his screen. He understands numbers much better than feelings, but he's trying hard to learn both. Logical, literal and very sweet: “Friendship can't be divided.”

THE ARTISTIC HEART · COLOUR & CREATIVITY

Arti & Pompita

“I paint the sky as colors fly.”

Arti is a tall, elegant unicorn whose mane changes colour with her mood, and whose horn can draw in the air. She sees the whole world as a canvas. Her best friend Pompita is a soft, pastel, floating little poop who sees the world as a happy place. Sensitive and creative, Arti believes: “If it's not perfect… it's even more beautiful.”
