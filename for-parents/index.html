What is Wacky Worlds?

Wacky Worlds is a series of activity books and stories set in Crazy Space Academy, a school floating in space where the most unusual students in the galaxy learn together. Each book follows one character and turns puzzles into missions, so every page feels like part of an adventure, not homework.

How the book works

10 missions

A story connects every challenge. Your child wakes the ship, survives an asteroid storm, decodes an alien message and earns the title of Master Pilot.

80+ puzzles

Seven challenges in every mission plus bonus puzzles, with a different kind of activity on every page, so there's real variety and no boredom.

Ages 6–8

110 pages of brain games, carefully balanced so kids feel proud and want to keep going. Answers are at the back of the book.

What it builds

  • Logic and thinking ahead: codes, sequences and patterns that activate reasoning without feeling like study.
  • Numbers: counting, simple operations and visual math puzzles.
  • Attention and observation: hidden objects, differences and matching.
  • Language: secret words, letters and clues to read and decode.
  • Confidence and focus: challenges that build pride instead of frustration, and deep focus with no screens at all.

Tips for using it at home

  • Little and often. One mission at a time works better than a marathon session.
  • Let them lead. If a challenge feels hard, skip it and come back later. The story will wait.
  • Celebrate each mission. At the end of the book there's a secret QR code that unlocks a printable Official Space Pilot Certificate.
  • Try before you buy. Print a free SUM-IX mission with three real pages from the book.

For teachers

The missions work well as warm-ups, early-finisher activities or a logic corner in the classroom. The free mission sample can be printed for your class. For anything else, such as group orders or questions about classroom use, get in touch.

Frequently asked questions

What age is it for?

Book 01 is designed for children aged 6 to 8. Younger kids can enjoy it with a grown-up's help; older kids often love the codes and logic challenges.

Does my child need to read well?

Instructions are short and simple. Early readers may need a hand with some of them, which is a great excuse to do the missions together.

Are the answers included?

Yes, all the answers are at the back of the book, so grown-ups can check without spoiling the fun.

Does it need screens or apps?

No. Everything happens on paper with a pencil. The only optional screen moment is scanning the QR code at the end to get the certificate.

Where can I buy it?

Book 01, SUM-IX and the Mystery of Pilot Zero, is available as a paperback on Amazon. See everything inside the book.
