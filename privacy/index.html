Privacy Policy

Plain and simple: what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Who we are

This website, wackyworldsbooks.com, belongs to Wacky Worlds Books, an independent children's book publisher based in Spain. We are the "data controller" of the personal data described here.

For anything related to your data, write to us at hello@wackyworldsbooks.com.

2. What we collect and why

When you ask for a free gift or join the Pilot Club

  • What: your email address, your consent to receive emails, and the date, time and technical details (such as IP address) of your sign-up and confirmation, which our email provider records as proof of consent.
  • Why: to send you the gift you asked for (for example, the Official Space Pilot Certificate) and, afterwards, occasional emails about Wacky Worlds: new books, free printables and activities.
  • Legal basis: your consent, which you give by ticking the box and confirming your email. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • How long: until you unsubscribe. After that we only keep the minimum needed to respect your choice (so we don't email you again) and to show that you had given consent.

When you write to us

If you email us or use the contact form, we use your name, email address and message only to answer you, and we keep them for as long as needed to handle your request. Messages sent through the contact form are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (see section 4).

When you just visit the website

We don't ask for any data. Like any website, the servers that deliver the pages may record technical data such as your IP address and browser type to keep the service running and secure (see section 4).

3. Children

Wacky Worlds books are for children, but this website and our emails are for parents and guardians over 18. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us their email, write to us and we will delete it.

4. Who else handles your data

We don't sell or rent your data. We only use trusted providers that help us run the website and send emails:

  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France): stores your email and sends our emails. Data is processed in the European Union.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): delivers the messages you send through our contact form to our email inbox.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA): hosts this website and may log technical data (such as IP addresses) when pages are visited.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC, USA): provides the typefaces used on this website, so your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with them.

Where a provider is outside the European Economic Area, transfers are protected by the safeguards the law requires, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Cookies

This website does not use cookies for advertising or tracking, and we don't use analytics tools. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

Our emails may include standard open and click measurement by Brevo, which helps us know whether our emails arrive and are useful.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • see the data we hold about you,
  • correct it,
  • delete it,
  • limit or object to how we use it,
  • receive a copy in a common format.

To unsubscribe from our emails, click "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any email. For anything else, write to hello@wackyworldsbooks.com and we'll reply within one month.

If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to a data protection authority. In Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es).

7. Security

The website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and access to our email list is protected with two-factor authentication.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date. If the changes are important, we will let you know by email.
