The mystery

SUM-IX is a little red robot with an LED chest full of numbers. He's brilliant at math, but flying a spaceship? That's a whole other story. To find out who Pilot Zero is, he needs a co-pilot: your child.

Together they wake a sleeping ship, survive an asteroid storm, decode an alien message and face the final trial. Every puzzle solved moves the story forward, so kids keep turning the page to see what happens next.

“I count and care, I solve and share.”

Questions

What age is it for?

Ages 6 to 8. Younger kids can enjoy it with a grown-up's help; older kids often love the codes and logic challenges.

Does my child need to read well?

Instructions are short and simple. Early readers may need a hand with some of them, which is a great excuse to do the missions together.

Are the answers included?

Yes, all the answers are at the back of the book.

Does it need screens or apps?

No. Everything happens on paper with a pencil. The only optional screen moment is scanning the QR code at the end to get the certificate.

Where can I buy it?
