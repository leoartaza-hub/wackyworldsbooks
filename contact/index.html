Before you write

I didn't receive my free gift or mission email

Check your spam or promotions folder for an email from hello@wackyworldsbooks.com. Remember to tap Confirm my email in the first email: the gift is sent right after that. Still nothing? Write to us and we'll sort it out.

How do I unsubscribe?

Click Unsubscribe at the bottom of any of our emails. It takes one click.

How do I get the Pilot Certificate?

Finish the missions in the book and scan the secret QR code on the last page.

Can I use the activities in my classroom?

Yes, the free mission sample can be printed for your class. For anything else, see For parents & teachers or write to us.
